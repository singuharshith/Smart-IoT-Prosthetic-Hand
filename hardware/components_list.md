# Component List

## Electronics

- Arduino UNO R3
- 5 x SG90 micro servo motors
- 5 x IR analog proximity sensors
- 5 x 3-pin female-to-male jumper wires
- Breadboard
- 7.4V battery pack or 5V DC regulated supply
- 5V power distribution rail or battery shield
- 4 x 100 nF decoupling capacitors (recommended)
- 10 kΩ potentiometer (optional)

## Mechanical

- 3D-printed prosthetic hand body
- Nylon thread or fishing line for tendon actuation
- Servo mounting brackets
- Fasteners and zip ties
- Rubber finger tips or flexible joint material

## Tools

- Arduino IDE
- USB cable for Arduino
- Digital multimeter
- Serial monitor / laptop
- Calibration rig or finger motion guide

## Notes

The project prioritizes mechanical stability and sensor alignment. For improved results, use a rigid hand frame and keep sensor distances consistent across each finger channel.
